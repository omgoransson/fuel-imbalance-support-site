Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Fuel Imbalance is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data collection

Fuel Imbalance does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or network services.

On-device storage

The app stores the most recently entered fuel target locally on your device so it is available when you next open the app. This value does not leave your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@omgmedia.se.
